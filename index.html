Method: what each calculation rests on
DRI feed rate.
Energy balance: feed (t/h) = arc power (kW) / effective melt energy (kWh/t), capped at the feeder maximum.
Energy to tap.
Remaining melt energy plus superheat (mass x heat capacity x degrees) minus the chemical credit from decarburization.
Oxygen volume.
Stoichiometry of C + O2, divided by utilization and O2 density; jet count fills the coherent flow band.
Carbon injection.
Slag-foaming practice: injection carbon proportional to simultaneous O2 flow.
Temperature.
Liquidus depression formula for C, Si, Mn, P, S; tap aim = liquidus + tundish superheat + ladle drop.
Slag formers.
Mass balance: SiO2 from DRI gangue plus oxidized Si, limed to the basicity target.
Alloys.
Element deficit x charge, divided by alloy grade x recovery.
Glossary
Tap.
Pouring the finished steel from the furnace into the ladle.
Tap aim.
The temperature the bath should reach at tap: liquidus plus tundish superheat plus ladle drop.
Liquidus.
The temperature where the steel is fully liquid, set by its chemistry.
On model.
Your actual reading is within 10% of what the model expects by now.
kWh/t.
Electrical energy per tonne of liquid steel.
DRI.
Direct reduced iron, fed continuously or charged in baskets.
Skull.
A build-up of frozen metal or unmelted material on the bottom or walls.
DRI pile-up.
Feeding DRI faster than it melts: a cone of unmelted material forms and can collapse into the bath.
Foamy slag.
Slag blown up with CO bubbles so it buries the arc and protects the walls.
Jets.
Supersonic oxygen jets. Each works well inside its own flow band.
Basicity (B2).
CaO divided by SiO2 in the slag.
