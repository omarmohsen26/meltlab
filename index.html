Numbers worth replacing with your plant's

  1. Lance specification: rated O2 flow and pressure per lance.
  2. Alloy grades and the recoveries you actually see for FeMn, FeSi, SiMn and coke.
  3. DRI certificate: gangue (SiO2), metallization and carbon - it sets the lime and energy numbers.
  4. Recent slag analyses (CaO, SiO2, FeO, MgO) to set basicity and MgO targets.
  5. Your furnace's power profile and transformer limits.
  6. Tap-to-tap times and electrode consumption for a few heats.
Before you rely on itEvery constant (oxygen factor and utilization, lance flow, alloy grades and recoveries, DRI gangue, basicity, superheat, ladle drop) is a textbook default, not your plant's number. Treat outputs as a structured second opinion until you replace the defaults with your own data.
