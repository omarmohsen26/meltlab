Numbers worth replacing with your plant's

  1. Effective DRI melt energy: back it out of your own heats as power (kW) divided by feed rate (t/h) while feeding steadily.
  2. Jet flow band: the lowest flow that keeps the jet coherent, and the nozzle design maximum.
  3. DRI certificate: gangue (SiO2), metallization and carbon - it sets the lime and melt-energy numbers.
  4. Recent slag analyses (CaO, SiO2, FeO, MgO) to set basicity and MgO targets.
  5. Alloy grades and the recoveries you actually see for FeMn, FeSi, SiMn and coke.
  6. Your transformer limit and tap program.
Before you rely on itEvery constant is a textbook default, not your plant's number. The model computes what an ideal heat would do; real heats carry losses it cannot see. Treat outputs as a structured second opinion until you replace the defaults with your own data - the qualified operator runs the heat, and plant procedures decide.
